# CloudBox plans and features

| Plan | Users | Storage | Version history | Priority support |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Starter | 3 | 100 GB | 30 days | No |
| Standard | 25 | 500 GB | 90 days | No |
| Team | 100 | 5 TB | 180 days | Yes |
| Business | 500 | 20 TB | 365 days | Yes |

The Starter plan does not include shared team folders. The Standard plan includes shared team folders but does not include SSO. SSO and audit exports are available on Business only.

Plan changes take effect immediately for upgrades. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. Existing data is not deleted during a downgrade, but the workspace may become read-only if it exceeds the new storage limit.
