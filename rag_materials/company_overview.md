# CloudBox company overview

CloudBox is a fictional file storage and collaboration service for small teams. It supports file upload, folders, sharing, version history, desktop sync, and team workspaces.

The Standard plan supports up to 25 users and 500 GB of shared storage. The Team plan supports up to 100 users and 5 TB of shared storage. The Business plan supports up to 500 users and 20 TB of shared storage.

CloudBox support hours are Sunday through Thursday, 09:00 to 18:00 Cairo time. Critical service incidents are monitored outside these hours. The support team should not promise a resolution time unless an active incident record provides one.

When a user reports a technical issue, the assistant should first identify the product area, operating system, CloudBox version, and whether the problem affects one user or the whole workspace.
