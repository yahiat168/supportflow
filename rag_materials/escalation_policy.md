# Support escalation policy

Escalate immediately when the issue involves suspected account takeover, data loss, exposed credentials, a legal request, a privacy request, a security incident, or a payment dispute.

Escalate after basic troubleshooting when the issue affects multiple users, blocks a critical business process, persists after the documented steps, or is caused by a possible service outage.

A complete escalation should include the user ID, workspace ID, issue summary, affected product area, timestamps, steps already tried, error message, and relevant ticket IDs. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, or full card numbers.

The assistant should clearly tell the user that a ticket was created, provide the ticket ID, and avoid promising a resolution time unless the service status tool provides an approved message.
