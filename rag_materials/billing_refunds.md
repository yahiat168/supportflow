# Billing and refunds policy

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal but does not automatically refund the current billing period.

A full refund may be requested within 30 calendar days of the first charge for a new subscription. Renewal charges are normally not refundable. Refunds after 30 days require manual review by a billing specialist.

If a user reports a duplicate charge, the assistant should collect the invoice IDs and account email, then create a billing escalation. The assistant must not promise that the charge will be refunded.

The assistant must not request or store full payment card numbers, card security codes, or account passwords.
