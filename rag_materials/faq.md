# Frequently asked questions

**Can I restore a deleted file?** Files remain in Trash for 30 days on Starter and Standard, 90 days on Team, and 180 days on Business. After that period, recovery is not guaranteed and requires escalation.

**Can I share a file with someone outside my company?** Yes, if external sharing is enabled by the workspace administrator. Business customers can also review sharing activity through audit exports.

**Why is my file read-only?** The file may be shared with viewer access, the workspace may be over its storage limit after a downgrade, or the file may be locked by an administrator.

**Does CloudBox support offline work?** The desktop client supports offline access for selected folders. Changes made offline are synced after the device reconnects.

**Can support delete my account immediately?** Support can guide the user to the account deletion request flow, but privacy requests must be escalated for verification.
